## 1. Peer-Reviewed Research Papers (2020–2025)

1. **Stamenković, D., Jovanović, Z., & Chakraborty, P. (2020)**  
   *Evaluation of simulation systems suitable for teaching compiler construction courses.*  
   *Computer Applications in Engineering Education*, 28(5), pp. 1160–1177.  
   **DOI:** [10.1002/cae.22231](https://doi.org/10.1002/cae.22231)  
   *Relevance:* Analyzes 15 educational compiler simulators and identifies the pedagogical necessity of end-to-end, multi-stage visual tools for computer science undergraduate courses.

2. **del Vado Vírseda, R. (2021)**  
   *Learning Compiler Design: From the Implementation to Theory.*  
   In *Proceedings of the 26th ACM Conference on Innovation and Technology in Computer Science Education (ITiCSE 2021)*, pp. 282–288.  
   **DOI:** [10.1145/3456565.3460041](https://doi.org/10.1145/3456565.3460041)  
   *Relevance:* Proposes an implementation-first methodology in compiler education, demonstrating that hands-on grammar construction and inspection reinforces formal language theory.

3. **del Vado Vírseda, R. (2022)**  
   *ITT: An Interactive Tutoring Tool to Improve the Learning and Visualization of Compiler Design Theory From Implementation.*  
   In *Proceedings of the 53rd ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education (SIGCSE 2022)*, pp. 696–702.  
   **DOI:** [10.1145/3478432.3499140](https://doi.org/10.1145/3478432.3499140)  
   *Relevance:* Demonstrates the educational efficacy of interactive tutoring workbenches in compiler construction and provides pedagogical evaluation metrics.

4. **Hiranishi, Y., et al. (2022)**  
   *Application of Web-based Visual Compiler to Computer Education.*  
   *IEEJ Transactions on Electronics, Information and Systems*, 142(4), pp. 389–396.  
   **DOI:** [10.1541/ieejeiss.142.389](https://doi.org/10.1541/ieejeiss.142.389)  
   *Relevance:* Details the design and student adoption of browser-based visual compiler interfaces to demonstrate instruction flow and state progression.

5. **Stamenković, D., & Jovanović, Z. (2024)**  
   *A Web-Based Educational System for Teaching Compilers.*  
   *IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies*, 17, pp. 819–830.  
   **DOI:** [10.1109/TLT.2023.3297626](https://doi.org/10.1109/TLT.2023.3297626)  
   *Relevance:* Presents empirical results showing that web-accessible compiler environments significantly increase student engagement and conceptual retention over terminal-only tools.

6. **Koitz-Hristov, R., Mandl, M., & Wotawa, F. (2025)**  
   *VisOpt – Visualization of Compiler Optimizations for Computer Science Education.*  
   In *Proceedings of the 56th ACM Technical Symposium on Computer Science Education (SIGCSE TS 2025)*, Pittsburgh, PA, USA.  
   **Venue:** ACM SIGCSE Technical Symposium 2025  
   *Relevance:* Focuses specifically on visual representations of compiler optimization passes, validating the pedagogical utility of side-by-side plan comparisons (original vs. optimized).

## 3. Standard Textbooks & Reference Works

8. **Aho, A. V., Lam, M. S., Sethi, R., & Ullman, J. D. (2006)**  
   *Compilers: Principles, Techniques, and Tools* (2nd ed.).  
   Boston, MA: Addison-Wesley (Dragon Book).  
   *Relevance:* Foundational reference for lexical analysis (DFA state machines), context-free grammars, LALR(1) shift-reduce parsing, AST structures, and symbol table design.

9. **Levine, J. (2009)**  
   *flex & bison: Text Processing Tools*.  
   Sebastopol, CA: O'Reilly Media.  
   *Relevance:* Practical reference for integrating Flex lexical scanners with Bison parsers in C/C++.

10. **Ramakrishnan, R., & Gehrke, J. (2003)**  
    *Database Management Systems* (3rd ed.).  
    New York, NY: McGraw-Hill.  
    *Relevance:* Core theory for relational algebra operators (Selection $\sigma$, Projection $\pi$, Ordering $\tau$), rule-based query rewriting, and physical relational plan execution.
